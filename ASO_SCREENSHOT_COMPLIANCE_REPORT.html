Verdict — do not upload this set to Production

This is not a set that needs polish. Three findings would, on their own, justify a suspension rather than a rejection, because each is a factual statement about the app that the app's own source code contradicts:

1 · "Your data stays on your device. We never send it to servers." — the coach is a server-side LLM. lib/features/coach/domain/coach_brain.dart calls a Supabase Edge Function (coach-chat) holding ANTHROPIC_API_KEY. The app's own localized string is honest — "Your training progress and profile details are stored on secure servers" — the screenshots are not.

2 · Every screenshot but two renders an iPhone, Dynamic Island included, in an Android listing.

3 · The Community screenshots depict a public text-and-photo feed that does not exist. squad_feed_screen.dart: "Three reactions and no text field … activity_reactions has no text column to moderate."

Add to that a store icon that reads YAPAY ("artificial / fake") in Turkish, a hero wordmark that spells the brand FORMI, and US/008.png being a byte-identical duplicate of US/007.png.

Upload blockers

before any policy question

These fail at the Play Console upload step or at the automated asset-quality gate — the human reviewer never gets to the creative. Fix these first; they are mechanical.

1 · Every screenshot carries an alpha channel — Play requires 24-bit PNG with no transparency

All 18 phone/tablet screenshots are RGBA. Play's spec is JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha. Flatten each against #08050E before export. (Both feature graphics and both icons are already RGB — correct.)

2 · Every screenshot is under 1080 px on its short side, and none is 9:16

Google requires at least four screenshots at minimum 1080 px with a 9:16 portrait ratio for the listing to qualify for promotion and featuring. Actual: 1024×1536 and 1023×1537 are 2:3 (0.666); 992×1586 is 0.625. Only 941×1672 (0.563) is near 9:16 — and it is under 1080 px wide. Nothing in the set qualifies.

Fix: regenerate or re-canvas every phone screenshot to 1080 × 1920 exactly. The GPT Image prompts below all specify 1080×1920; if your generator will not emit that ratio, generate at 1024×1536 and re-compose onto a 1080×1920 canvas in a design tool — do not upscale-stretch.

3 · US/008.png is a byte-identical duplicate of US/007.png

md5 = d5d71539720a3d9ef8ccf6b0361da551 for both. The US listing therefore ships 7 unique screenshots, one of them twice, and the Body Metrics / Trends asset is missing entirely. Duplicate carousel slides are a documented asset-quality rejection reason and they waste the single most valuable slot in the set.

4 · The two locales are not "identical except language" — the sets have diverged

The brief assumed parity. They do not have it:

  • TUR/007.png is a Challenges & Leaderboard screenshot that has no US equivalent — the US slot is the duplicate.
  • US/005.png carries a bottom trust bar ("100% PRIVACY · 7 DAYS FREE · REAL PEOPLE — Join thousands of motivated members") that TUR/005.png does not have at all.
  • TUR/001 spells the shirt PERSONAL TRAINER; TUR/003 spells it PERSONEL TRAINER. Same garment, same set, two spellings.
  • Dimensions differ per slot within each set (941×1672 vs 1023×1537), so the carousel will letterbox inconsistently.

A reviewer comparing locales sees an inconsistent product. Decide the canonical 8 and mirror them exactly.

5 · Two different app icons were produced — Play has exactly one, and it is not localizable

US/app-icon.png reads AI FITNESS COACH; TUR/store-app-icon.png reads YAPAY | FİTNESS KOÇUNUZ. Screenshots and the feature graphic are per-locale; the 512×512 icon is global. One of these must be discarded — and as it happens, both must be, for the reasons in their own sections below.

Section C · Optimization suggestions

not required for approval

Nothing here affects approval. All of it affects whether the listing converts once approved.

C1 · Lead with the one thing only this app can honestly show

Screenshots 1–3 are what appear in search results, and they are the only ones most people ever see. US/006 and TUR/006 — the live pose skeleton — are the single most differentiated assets in the set and they are sitting in slot 6. Every competitor can show a plan, a recipe list and a chat bubble. Almost none can show joint-level form analysis running on the handset. Move it to slot 1 in both locales.

Suggested order: 1 Form detection · 2 AI coach chat · 3 30-day plan · 4 Progress · 5 Nutrition · 6 Challenges · 7 Community · 8 Body metrics.

C2 · Every asset is a poster; almost none survives thumbnail size

US/001 carries roughly 24 discrete text elements. In the Play carousel it renders about 25 mm wide, where none of them is readable. The rule that works: one headline, one screen, at most two callouts. Everything beyond that is decoration that costs legibility. The corrected prompts above already reduce card counts; resist adding them back.

C3 · The disclaimer strip is an asset, not a liability

US/007 and TUR/008 show a permanent, honest disclosure — "General fitness advice only, not medical advice" — inside the product UI. Most AI fitness apps hide this. Showing it converts: it is the clearest possible signal that the product is built by people who thought about the responsibility. Keep it prominent, and consider making the same point in the long description.

C4 · Camera privacy is your strongest honest claim — so state it precisely

"Camera footage never leaves your device" is true, verifiable and genuinely rare. The set currently dilutes it into "100% PRIVACY, all data stays on your device", which is false — and a false absolute destroys the credibility of the true specific. Narrow every privacy line to the camera, and it becomes both compliant and more persuasive.

C5 · Rest days differentiate; "no skipping" does not

The market is saturated with apps that pretend you train daily. FormAI schedules rest — that is a mature product decision and a reason to trust it. "30 days. 24 sessions. Six rest days, scheduled for you." is both more honest and more appealing than "30 days. 30 sessions. No excuses."

C6 · The US listing is a translated Turkish listing, and it shows

Turkish percent notation, European decimals, Turkish names in the community feed, Turkish cuisine as the nutrition hook, Turklish dish names ("Chicken Bulgurlu Salad"). A US shopper reads a regional product. Either localize the US assets properly — Western dishes, US names, US number formats — or focus the launch on TR and treat en-US as a secondary listing until it can be done well.

C7 · Cast people the audience can see themselves in

Every human figure across 22 assets is a young, lean, muscular man. The nutrition, plan and progress features have no gender skew, and roughly half the addressable market is being shown a product that is not for them. Mixed casting across the carousel costs nothing and widens the funnel — it also softens the body-image exposure in B11.

C8 · Ship 8 screenshots, and make them the same 8 in both locales

Play allows 8; more is more surface. But the current sets carousel different features per locale. Decide the canonical eight, mirror them exactly, and keep the ordering identical so the listings read as one product.

C9 · Consider a promo video — but only after the feature graphic is fixed

A 30-second capture of the pose skeleton counting reps would outperform any static asset. Note the dependency: attaching a video makes Play overlay a play button on the centre 250×250 px of the feature graphic, which is currently full of hologram and trainer. The corrected feature-graphic prompt already reserves that zone.
