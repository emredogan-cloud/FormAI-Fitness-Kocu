FormAI — Play Store ASO Image Prompt Library

GPT Image prompts for every Google Play graphic FormAI needs, plus optional promotional assets. Each card is self-contained: copy the prompt, generate, check it against the restrictions in the same card. Written against build 1.0.0+38 · package com.emredogan.formaifit.

Non-negotiable rules — these came from a Play policy audit, not from taste

  • Never put an outcome promise in an asset. No “transform in 30 days”, no “lose X kg”, no before/after body comparison. The app’s own copy was stripped of exactly these claims in build 38 — a store asset that still makes them contradicts the product and is a Misrepresentation risk.
  • Never invent social proof: no star ratings, no “★ 4.9”, no download counts, no testimonials, no press logos, no “#1 fitness app”. FormAI has no such numbers and fabricating them is a straightforward policy violation.
  • Never render an iPhone, an iOS status bar, a notch or a Dynamic Island. Android device frames only. iOS frames in a Play listing were flagged in the audit as an asset-quality/deception risk.
  • Never show a feature that does not ship: no smartwatch pairing, no heart-rate/BPM readout, no calorie-burn-from-camera, no live video calls with a human trainer, no barcode food scanning, no Apple Health/Google Fit sync. FormAI has none of these.
  • Never draw a fake Google Play badge, a fake “Install”/“Get” button, the Google logo, or Play’s own UI chrome inside an asset. Play rejects assets that imitate store UI.
  • Always keep the AI claim honest: pose/form analysis runs on-device (Google ML Kit); the chat coach is a server-side LLM. Never write “100% on-device AI” — that was corrected in the app and must not come back through an image.
  • Always spell it PERSONAL TRAINER if that phrase appears. The shipped hero art contains the typo PERSONEL TRAINER; every regenerated asset must fix it.
  • Always use American English in EN assets (“program”, not “programme”) and correct Turkish typography in TR assets (ı İ ş ğ ç ö ü). Generated art frequently mangles Turkish diacritics — inspect every glyph.
  • Always treat generated text as unreliable. GPT Image misspells. Either request minimal text and typeset the real copy afterwards in a design tool, or proofread every character at 100% zoom before upload.
  • Always match the in-app palette below so the store page and the app read as one product.

Rules that apply to all eight screenshots

  • Safe areas (identical on every screenshot): keep the marketing headline inside the top 300 px, keep the device mockup between y ≈ 340 px and y ≈ 1780 px, and leave 72 px clear on the left and right and 90 px clear at the bottom. Play’s carousel rounds the corners and crops a few pixels.
  • The UI inside the frame must be the real app. The most reliable method is: capture a real screenshot on the device, then use the prompt below to generate only the surrounding scene (frame, background, glow, floating cards) and composite the real capture into the frame.   • No fabricated data. Streaks, XP, calorie numbers and chart shapes shown in a screenshot must be plausible for a real early user. Do not show “Day 247” or “15,000 XP” on a launch listing.
