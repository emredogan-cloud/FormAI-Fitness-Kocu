2 · Remaining issues

4 blocking · 5 minor · 1 export setting

BLOCKER 1 · The app icon — the corrective prompt was not applied

The prompt specified a flat vector F monogram, full-bleed, no rounding, no border, no human figures. What was produced is two neon wireframe human bodies. Four separate problems:

  • Pre-rounded corners with a glowing border, on a black square. Play applies its own rounded mask and shadow, so this gets masked twice — the border will be clipped into four disconnected arcs and the black corners will show as a hard square behind the mask.
  • Illegible at 48 px. Downscaled, the two figures collapse into an indistinct purple-and-green smear. You cannot tell what the app is. This is the size the icon renders at in search results and the app drawer.
  • Nude anatomical wireframes. Both figures are rendered as unclothed body forms with contour detail. At icon scale in a fitness listing this is an avoidable content-rating question.
  • It reads as body scanning. Wireframe bodies with node points imply body-composition analysis — a softer version of the exact health-claim imagery the previous audit flagged in the old icon.

The icon is a required asset. This one cannot ship. Regenerate with the monogram prompt from the previous report.

BLOCKER 2 · TUR/NEW/006.png — Squad content pasted into the Form Detection asset

The top feature row reads KÜÇÜK TAKIMLAR / SIRALAMA DEĞİL, KATILIM / VARSAYILAN GİZLİ — verbatim from TUR/NEW/005.png. It is not a policy violation, it is a content-integrity defect, and it is plainly visible: the screenshot contradicts its own headline. Replace that row with the camera-feature columns (ON-DEVICE AI / GERÇEK ZAMANLI POZ ANALİZİ / SESLİ KOÇLUK), or delete the row entirely — the four side cards already carry those messages, and the English twin has no top row at all.

BLOCKER 3 · Both tablet assets still misrepresent the app

US/NEW/009.png and TUR/NEW/009(useless).png depict a nav rail, a two-up workout grid and a category taxonomy under a headline claiming the app is built for large screens. Verified again: the only width-responsive consumer layout in lib/ does not exist — the ≥600 px branch lives in admin_dashboard_screen.dart. The Turkish one additionally states %60 for 12 of 30 days, which is 40%. Delete both. Play requires no tablet screenshot, and the folder name suggests this call was already made.

BLOCKER 4 · Alpha channel — a hard upload blocker on all 18 screenshots

Every phone screenshot in both NEW folders is still RGBA. Play requires JPEG or 24-bit PNG with no alpha. Flatten against #08050E on export. The feature graphics and the icon are already RGB and correct.

  • US/005 — "naximum" → "maximum".
  • US/004 — "9 days left" / "18 days left" are stacked beside Day 30 instead of one per milestone row; streak shows 7 dots for a 6-day streak.
  • TUR/004 — the pill renders "Β0 Günlük Plan" instead of "30"; streak shows 8 dots for a 6-day streak.

Long Description2,457 / 4,000Play limit: 4,000 characters
FormAI turns your phone camera into a form check.

Prop your phone up, start a set, and FormAI reads your body position while you move — counting reps and calling out cues in real time. The pose analysis runs on your device. Camera frames are processed on your phone and are never uploaded.

WHAT YOU GET

▸ REAL-TIME FORM ANALYSIS
Joint-level pose tracking, live rep counting and a form score for the set. Spoken cues as you move, so you are not staring at the screen mid-squat.

▸ A 30-DAY PLAN BUILT FROM YOUR ANSWERS
Tell FormAI your goal, your experience level, how long you can train and what equipment you have. You get a 30-day program with rest days already scheduled — 24 sessions, not 30, because recovery is part of training.

▸ AN AI COACH THAT REPLIES
Ask about your plan, your form, your next session, recovery or nutrition. Replies are produced by an AI model and are general fitness guidance, not medical advice. Every message can be flagged in one tap.

▸ 250+ RECIPES WITH THE MACROS ALREADY COUNTED
Filter by your goal and your dietary preferences. Calories, protein, carbs and fat are calculated for every recipe. Export a shopping list from the ones you favourite.

▸ PROGRESS YOU CAN ACTUALLY READ
Streaks, XP, badges and weekly consistency. Log your weight and five tape measurements, then see the trend across 7, 30 or 90 days. You enter the numbers — the app shows the line, without a verdict attached.

▸ SQUADS OF TWELVE
Join a small squad and see who showed up. Presence, not ranking: three reactions, no comment box, nobody placed above anybody. Your profile stays private until you choose to share it.

▸ CHALLENGES AND FAIR RANKINGS
Time-boxed challenges that rotate each month, with leaderboards scoped to your level so beginners are not measured against veterans.

PRIVACY, STATED PLAINLY
Camera footage never leaves your device — form analysis runs entirely on your phone and no image is recorded or sent anywhere. Your training history, profile and chat are stored on secure servers, tied to your account and sent over an encrypted connection. We tell you which is which because the difference matters.

WHAT FORMAI IS NOT
FormAI is a training tool, not a medical device and not a clinician. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it makes no promise about how your body will change. Train sensibly, and if something hurts, stop and speak to a qualified professional.
